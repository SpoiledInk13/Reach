# Storybook artwork

Five original AI-generated illustrations made with the built-in imagegen tool for
the Reach presentation. The first scene served as the visual reference for each
subsequent image; no slides or text are baked into the artwork.

## Character and setting

Pip is a small human adventurer with tousled brown hair, a rust-red cape, moss-green
tunic, brown boots, and tan satchel. The cottage has cream plaster, a stone base,
moss-green roof, brick chimney, arched oak door on the left, and a low casement
window with teal shutters on the right. Inside is a warm stone fireplace.

The style is painted gouache with pencil texture, warm paper, muted woodland
colors, and blue dusk outside. The presentation uses that palette with Georgia
headings and Calibri body text. Green identifies ideate, rust identifies build,
and gold identifies milestone; text labels also identify each skill.

| File | Story purpose |
|---|---|
| `pip-cottage-open.png` | Master reference: Pip's destination and the open route |
| `pip-paperwork.png` | Process growing larger than the playable goal |
| `pip-door-closed.png` | A blocked route and an unanswered design question |
| `pip-window-open.png` | A locked door and another usable kind of opening |
| `pip-fireplace.png` | The experience reaching its intended outcome |

All five images were reviewed for character, palette, setting, and the relevant
door/window state. The images are fictional illustrations, not game screenshots.
Original generated assets are retained here at their native resolution. Slides
place them without cropping so Pip and the entrances remain visible.
